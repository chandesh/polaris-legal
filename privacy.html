Polaris

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 17, 2026

Short version: Polaris collects nothing. Your entries, tags, insights, routines, and settings are stored only on your device. There is no account, no server, no analytics, and no advertising. A copy of your data leaves your device only when you explicitly export a backup file yourself.

Who we are

Polaris is an independently developed iPhone app. This policy explains what happens to information when you use it. You can reach us through the support page.

What Polaris stores, and where

Everything you create in Polaris - the things you notice, the tags you add, any mood, energy, or sleep you record, your routine blocks and their logs, and your settings - is stored in a database on your device. It is not uploaded anywhere.

Polaris does not ask you to create an account and does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, or any device identifier.

What Polaris does not collect

  • No personal information and no account.
  • No analytics, telemetry, or crash reporting.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No usage tracking across apps or websites.
  • No location data.

Backups and exports

You can export a backup of your data from Settings. When you do, Polaris writes a single JSON file and hands it to the standard iOS share sheet, where you choose what to do with it (for example, save it to Files). That file contains your data in plain text. Once it leaves the app, it is under your control and subject to whatever destination you choose - for example, iCloud Drive or another app. Polaris itself never uploads it and keeps no copy.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, they are scheduled locally by your device. There is no push service. Reminder settings are stored on your device only.

Face ID and device passcode

If you enable the app lock, Polaris asks iOS to authenticate you using Face ID or your device passcode. Polaris never sees or stores your biometric data, and it cannot access it. Authentication is handled entirely by the operating system.

Children's privacy

Polaris is suitable for a general audience and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Third parties

Polaris does not share data with third parties, because it does not collect any. There are no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services in the app.

Your control over your data

Because your data lives only on your device, you are always in control of it. You can export a backup at any time, and you can erase everything from within the app (Settings, then Delete all data). Deleting the app also removes its data from your device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Reach us through the support page.
