Frequently asked

Where is my data stored? Is it private?

Everything stays on your iPhone. Polaris works fully offline, has no account, and never sends your data anywhere. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I back up or restore my data?

Open Settings, then Your data. Export writes a backup file and opens the iOS share sheet, where you can save it to Files (or send it wherever you like). Import lets you pick a backup file to restore. Keep your backup file somewhere safe - there is no cloud copy.

How do I erase everything?

Settings, then Your data, then Delete all data. This is permanent. Polaris offers to export a backup first. Deleting the app also removes its data from your device.

Notifications are not arriving

Check that notifications are allowed for Polaris in iOS Settings, Notifications, Polaris. In the app, confirm the reminder is switched on under Settings, Reminders, and that its time is what you expect.

I forgot my app lock / it will not unlock

The app lock uses your device Face ID or passcode, so it unlocks the same way your phone does. If you cannot authenticate, unlock your device first, then reopen Polaris.

Does Polaris need an internet connection?

No. Polaris is designed to work completely offline. It does not require network access to function.
